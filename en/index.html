Sheet 01 · Situation39.5696° N · 2.6502° E

Where I stand and where I look from

Community nursing with epidemiological methods: the neighbourhood as a determinant of health.

I hold a PhD from the Universitat de les Illes Balears (2021, summa cum laude) and have taught on the Bachelor's Degree in Nursing since 2020. I apply epidemiological methods to questions from community nursing and primary care: how the neighbourhood —its walkability, its green spaces, the air we breathe— shapes physical activity, obesity and cardiometabolic health across the life course.

I work with populations under nursing care: older adults at high cardiovascular risk in the PREDIMED-Plus trial, children, and patients recruited in primary care centres. I lead two lines using real-world data from IB-SALUT electronic health records —a childhood obesity observatory and small-area paediatric cancer risk— and I am principal investigator of PRE-ENVIRONS (ISCIII).

I use geographic information systems (ArcGIS, QGIS) and R (multilevel models, GAM/GAMM, Bayesian inference). I speak Catalan (B2) and English (C1).
